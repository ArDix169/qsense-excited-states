USE THE 2-TUPLE KEY.  (irrep, spin) is the FULL active space; the
3-tuple ...,'cas' is the frozen-core sector.  Q-SENSE's actmo_start
restricts which excitations the ANSATZ generates, not the Hamiltonian,
so full-space FCI is what the ansatz approximates.  Differencing
against the frozen-core sector understates the error by ~0.15 mHa and
can produce a NEGATIVE dE, which is variationally impossible.
